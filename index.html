Academic Research

M.Sc. in Data Science · Awarded 2026

Applying AI Tools to Improve Current Actuarial Pricing and Risk Models Using Wearable Devices Data

Cairo University — Faculty of Graduate Studies for Statistical Research, Department of Data Science.
Supervisors: Prof. Abdelhadi Nabih Ahmed and Prof. Mohamed Reda Abonazel.

  • Built and validated mortality-risk models on the U.S. National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES 2003–2006), using held-out samples fixed in advance.
  • Showed that a biomarker-based biological-age model (Phenotypic Age) ranked held-out mortality risk better than chronological age, while its raw probabilities required recalibration.
  • Compared observed and expected deaths under United States and Egyptian life tables, linking statistical models to actuarial pricing practice.
